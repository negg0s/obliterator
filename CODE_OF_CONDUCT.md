# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We are committed to providing a welcoming, productive, and harassment-free environment for all contributors and users.

We expect all participants to:
- Be respectful, considerate, and professional.
- Focus on technical merit, quality, and safety.
- Offer and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Show empathy and patience toward community members.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, personal attacks, and disruptive conduct.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior. Maintainers have the right to edit, reject, or close comments, issues, and pull requests that do not adhere to this Code of Conduct.
